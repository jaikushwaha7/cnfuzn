Five doors, one engine

Pick the door that fits your problem.

Every confusion reduces to a few questions. Each needs a different tool, and using the wrong tool is why people stay stuck.

01 · WHAT IS TRUE?

Knowledge

Evidence ledger with a Bayesian update and bias check. The only place we use probability, because facts have odds.

02 · WHAT DO I WANT?

Preference

Five Whys plus an emotion check: is this a want or a reaction?

03 · WHAT SHOULD I DO?

Decision & morals

Options weighed by ability to act, reversibility and values, not by guessing outcomes.

04 · WHO AM I?

Identity

Values plus an inherited-versus-chosen audit across gender, race, caste, religion, culture and class.

05 · WHERE AM I GOING?

Direction

A small Plan, Do, Check, Act experiment with a review date.

Confusion isn't only human: a system meeting several possible states with incomplete information must respond, adapt or evolve. Microbe: signal → sensing → response. Animal: senses → nervous system → choice. Human: information + memory + emotion + values → decision. Society: information + interests + institutions → collective decision. People add values and meaning, so this engine never stops at facts.
